About Haulway Heroes

Haulway Heroes is a 3D truck-driving career. You start in Port Bayu with a small cab-over truck and $6,000, pick contracts from the job board and drive real roads with traffic lights and toll gates.

Each job ends by reversing your trailer into a loading bay. Pay adds up base pay, time, cargo damage, parking and comfort bonuses, minus fines. There are 8 cities and 7 trucks, opening as you level up.

3 quick tips

  1. Keep to the speed limit, stop at red lights and slow down for toll gates to avoid fines.
  2. A fast but messy delivery can pay less than a slow, clean one.
  3. When docking goes wrong, drive forward a little to straighten up and try again.

How to drive

  • Steer with the wheel (bottom left), ◀ ▶ buttons or by tilting — choose in Settings.
  • GAS and BRAKE pedals are bottom right. Tap D/R to switch between drive and reverse (stop first).
  • Lane Assist keeps you in lane when you let go of the wheel. Trailer Assist: in reverse, steering aims the trailer.
  • Obey speed limits, red lights and speed cameras. Slow down at toll gates and stop on the weigh station scale.
  • Watch fuel ⛽ and damage 🔧. Stop on a gas station pad to refuel. Fragile cargo hates bumps!
  • At the depot, back the trailer into the green dock. Hold still for a second to finish. AUTO-PARK helps (smaller bonus).
  • Earn money and XP, buy trucks, trailers and upgrades, unlock new cities.
  • Keyboard: arrows/WASD, Space handbrake, R reverse, H horn, L lights, Q/E indicators, C camera, P pause.
